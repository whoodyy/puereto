Welcome

Three villas, one private resort

Beside St. Paul College in Pantubig, San Rafael, Bulacan, Puerto de San Rafael is a place for family gatherings, barkada outings and big celebrations. Each villa is sized for a different group.

Your escape, your pace

Cool off in the pool under the shade net, play a round of billiards, or take the mic on the videoke.

Every villa has a kitchen with a refrigerator, rice cooker and BBQ grill, so you can cook your own meals and gather in the evening.
